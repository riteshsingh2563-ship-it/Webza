## 2. Logo Geometry & Geometry Rules
- **Emblem**:
  - The stylized geometric "W".
  - Left & center strokes: Heavy architectural black angular diagonals with crisp 60°/120° facets.
  - Right arm: Distinctive horizontal base shelf with a floating upper-right chevron/parallelogram rendered in signature **Muted Olive Green** (`#6B7D50`).
- **Wordmark**:
  - `WEBZA` in custom bold architectural sans-serif.
  - Split color: `WEB` in deep obsidian (`#0E110E`), `ZA` in muted olive (`#6B7D50`).
- **Tagline Placement**:
  - `BUILT TO BE SEEN` centered directly beneath with letter spacing `0.35em`.

## 4. Typography Scale & Pairings
- **Display Headings**: `Space Grotesk` (Weights: 600, 700)
- **Editorial Contrast Accent**: `Instrument Serif` / `Playfair` (Weight: 400 italic) - used sparingly for editorial punch (e.g. "Your Business Deserves a *Better* Web.")
- **Body & Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 400, 500, 600)
- **Engineering Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 400, 500) for metrics, speeds, code snippets, and budget calculations.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Explicitly Forbidden)
- ❌ Generic AI template SaaS layouts (wavy purple gradients, generic cartoon illustrations).
- ❌ Bootstrap-style button clusters and standard table layouts.
- ❌ Unrelated neon colors (bright cyan, hot pink, orange, yellow).
- ❌ Generic stock photo people in fake office meetings.
- ❌ Cluttered animations that violate `prefers-reduced-motion`.
